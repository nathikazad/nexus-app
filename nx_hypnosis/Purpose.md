# NX Hypnosis

## Purpose

NX Hypnosis is a mobile app for listening to immersive stories narrated in a calming, hypnotic voice. Each story follows a protagonist whose choices and actions embody values and mindsets the listener personally wants to cultivate.

The aim is to make those values feel concrete, relatable, and emotionally meaningful through story. The listener encounters someone practicing a chosen value in everyday situations: noticing opportunities, making decisions, meeting setbacks, and following through. The experience is intended to support reflection and the listener's own practice of those values.

## The central idea

The listener chooses what matters to them. The app turns that intention into a story in which the protagonist demonstrates it through behavior. Gentle narration, deliberate pacing, and thoughtful pauses create a relaxed, absorbing listening experience.

For example, a listener might choose an abundance mentality in wealth creation. A story could follow a protagonist who notices an unmet need, creates something useful, collaborates generously, and learns from an unsuccessful attempt. The mindset appears in what the protagonist does and how they respond, with realistic effort and uncertainty along the way.

Wealth creation is only one example. Other themes could include courage, patience, creativity, self-respect, generosity, or consistency. The listener defines the values; the app does not prescribe a single ideal way to live.

## How the experience would work

1. **Choose an intention.** The listener describes a value or mindset they want to strengthen, optionally adding a situation in which they want to practice it.
2. **Shape the story.** The listener can choose preferences such as setting, protagonist, tone, and session length. Personal context is optional.
3. **Create the narrative.** The app develops a coherent story around the intention. The protagonist faces meaningful choices and demonstrates the selected value through specific actions, including how they handle difficulty.
4. **Narrate gently.** The story is delivered in a warm, hypnotic voice with a comfortable pace, natural pauses, and consistent volume. Voice and pacing preferences should be adjustable.
5. **Listen with control.** The listener can pause, resume, replay, or stop at any time. Sessions begin with a gentle settling-in passage and close with a clear, unhurried ending.
6. **Reflect if desired.** An optional closing prompt invites the listener to identify one small action inspired by the story. Favorite stories can be saved and revisited.

## Story and voice principles

- Show the chosen value through the protagonist's decisions, actions, and consequences.
- Give the protagonist believable motivations, challenges, and room to grow.
- Keep the narrative engaging in its own right, with sensory detail and a satisfying arc.
- Make the spoken experience continuous: move gently from relaxation into imagery and the protagonist’s experience without announcing that a story is beginning or explaining the product mechanics.
- Use encouraging, invitational language that respects the listener's agency.
- Treat “hypnotic” as a description of the relaxed, immersive delivery. The product should not promise guaranteed subconscious change or therapeutic outcomes.
- Keep personalization explicit and under the listener's control, including the ability to edit or remove personal context.

## Initial product scope

The first version should establish one complete experience: enter a value, create a story centered on it, listen to the narration, and save or replay the session. A simple player and a small personal story library are enough to begin.

Story generation and voice synthesis are the two main capabilities to explore. Technology choices, providers, storage, and integration with the existing mobile apps will be decided during implementation. This document establishes the product direction; it does not imply those capabilities have already been built.

## What success looks like

The listener enjoys the story, recognizes their chosen value in the protagonist's behavior, and finishes with a clearer sense of how they might embody it in their own life. Feedback on relevance, narration comfort, and willingness to listen again should guide improvements.
